Please read this before using Nexus for the first time.

1. What Nexus is

Nexus is a local student planner. It runs on your device and is provided as-is for personal, educational use.

2. Your data stays on this device

Classes, tasks, notes, wallet, events, and passwords are stored in this browser/app only. There is no cloud account. Uninstalling the app, clearing site data, or switching devices can erase everything unless you export a backup.

3. Passwords are not encrypted

The Password Manager stores entries on this device in readable form. Do not store passwords here if others can access this phone. Keep the device locked.

4. Notifications

Reminders only work if you grant notification permission. Delivery is not guaranteed when the app is closed or the system restricts background activity.

5. Accuracy

You are responsible for the information you enter (schedules, amounts, due dates). Nexus does not replace official school records or financial advice.

6. Backups

Export regularly from Settings → Backup. We cannot recover data you did not export.

7. Age

If you are under 18, use Nexus with a parent or guardian’s permission where required.

8. Changes

These terms may be updated in future versions. Continued use after an update means you accept the new terms.

You can read these again anytime in Settings.
